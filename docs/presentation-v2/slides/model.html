Slide 6 (1.5 minutes). Read the diagram from the top. A product belongs to a category. A ledger entry records which product, which kind of movement, optionally which transfer it belongs to, plus the warehouse and the user who did it, and users have roles. The two dashed tables at the bottom are derived: stock_balance holds the current quantity per product and warehouse, and daily_stock_summary holds in and out per day. Triggers keep them in step, and they can be rebuilt from the ledger. Mention the design is in BCNF: every fact lives in one place.
