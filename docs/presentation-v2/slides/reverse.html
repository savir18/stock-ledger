Moving stock and undoing a mistake both work by adding entries.

Slide 11 (1 minute). Two everyday operations, both just rows. A transfer between warehouses is one minus row in the source and one plus row in the destination, tied together by a transfer id, written in a single transaction so you can never lose stock in transit. A correction is a reversal row: it has the opposite quantity and points at the original. The original stays visible, which is the point: the trail is complete. In the demo, the History page strikes through the cancelled entry and shows the new reversal row.
