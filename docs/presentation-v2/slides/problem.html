Slide 2 (1 minute). Picture a spreadsheet cell holding 120. Someone types 80. The 120 is gone, and nothing records who did it or why. That is the first problem, no trail. The second is no proof: you cannot show that today's total equals what really happened. The third is races: two people can both sell the last unit if the system reads the number, then writes it back. Keep it conversational and avoid jargon.
