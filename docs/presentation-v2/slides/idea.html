Slide 3 (1 minute). Explain it like a bank statement. Every movement is a new row: a purchase, a sale, a transfer. The quantity on hand is just the sum of the rows. If an entry was wrong, you add a cancelling row; you never edit or delete the old one. And because balances come from the rows, they can always be rebuilt and proven.
