Slide 3 (1 minute). Compare the two models. On the left, one number that gets edited. On the right, a ledger where every movement is its own row and the quantity on hand is simply the sum. If a sale was a mistake, you do not delete it; you add a cancelling entry, and both stay visible. The single rule of the whole project is: you may add a row, you may not change or remove one. Everything else follows from that rule.
