Recording and reviewing changes

Record a change shows stock before and after you save. MySQL still has the final say.

History keeps every entry. A cancelled one is struck through and a new row is added.

Slide 17 (1 minute). Left: Record a change. The page is written in plain words, for example Sold to a customer, and shows the stock before and after as a bar so the user sees the effect before pressing Save. If the quantity is too large the bar turns red, and if it is saved anyway MySQL refuses it. Right: History. Every entry is listed. A cancelled entry is struck through, and the cancelling row appears next to it, which is the immutable ledger made visible.
