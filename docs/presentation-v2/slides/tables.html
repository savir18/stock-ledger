All twelve tables, in one line each

The filled card is the source of truth. Dashed cards are derived from it.

role

The four job types.

app_user

People who sign in, each with one role.

category

Product groups. They can nest.

supplier

Who stock is bought from.

warehouse

A storage location.

product

An item, with SKU, cost and reorder level.

product_supplier

Which supplier sells which product.

txn_type

Kinds of movement, and whether each adds or removes.

stock_transfer

Links the two rows of one transfer.

stock_ledger

One row per change. Never edited.

stock_balance

Current quantity per product and warehouse.

daily_stock_summary

Stock in and out per day, for analytics.

Slide 7 (1 minute). A quick tour of all twelve tables. The first five describe the business: roles, users, categories, suppliers, warehouses. Then product and the link between products and suppliers. txn_type lists the kinds of movement and whether each adds or removes stock. stock_transfer ties together the two rows of a transfer. stock_ledger is the one that matters. The last two are derived copies, kept for speed, that can always be rebuilt.
