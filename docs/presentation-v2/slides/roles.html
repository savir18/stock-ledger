Slide 21 (45 seconds). The four roles and what each can do. Admin can do everything and uses the Safety checks, Speed lab and Admin pages. The Warehouse Manager handles sales, returns, write-offs and transfers. The Purchase Officer posts purchases and returns to suppliers. The Analyst can look but cannot post anything. In the demo, signing in as Analyst shows that the Record a change page is not even offered, and a forced call is refused by the database.
