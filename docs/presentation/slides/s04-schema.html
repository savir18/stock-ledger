Slide 4 (about 60 seconds). Show the schema. Left: nine ordinary master-data tables, normalized to BCNF. Centre: the ledger, the only table that records history. Right: two derived tables kept up to date by a trigger so reads are fast. Point out the two arrows: master data is referenced by the ledger, and the ledger feeds the derived tables. Mention that the ledger has no foreign keys because of partitioning, and a trigger validates every reference instead. In the live demo this schema is visible with SHOW TABLES and DESCRIBE stock_ledger.
