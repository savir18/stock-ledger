Rules inside the database

Four triggers guard the ledger

trg_ledger_no_update

BEFORE UPDATE

Raises SQLSTATE 45000: “stock_ledger is append-only”. Add a reversing entry instead.

trg_ledger_no_delete

BEFORE DELETE

Same refusal for DELETE. Even the administrator cannot rewrite history.

trg_ledger_bi

BEFORE INSERT

Validates every reference, the sign against the type (a sale must be negative), transfer legs and reversal rules.

trg_ledger_ai

AFTER INSERT

Updates stock_balance and daily_stock_summary inside the same transaction.

Known gap: TRUNCATE and DROP do not fire triggers. Privileges prevent them and binary logs recover from them (slide 12).

Slide 6 (about 60 seconds). This is where “immutable” is enforced by MySQL itself rather than by the application. Two triggers refuse UPDATE and DELETE with SIGNAL. One validates every insert, replacing the foreign keys we cannot have. One keeps the summary tables in sync. Be upfront about the limit: TRUNCATE and DROP bypass triggers, which is why the app account has no such privileges and why recovery (slide 12) exists.
