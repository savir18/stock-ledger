Slide 3 (about 45 seconds). This is the core idea, borrowed from accounting: you never erase an entry, you add a correcting one. Walk through the four rows: a purchase of 50, two sales, then a reversal that cancels the last sale. The balance is just the running sum. For the judge: think of it as an event-sourced table, with the quantity on hand being a fold over the events.
