Slide 12 (about 45 seconds). Closing the gap from slide 6. TRUNCATE and DROP skip triggers, so the defence is a backup plus the binary log. Take a mysqldump with flush-logs, which starts a new log, and source-data=2, which records the log position inside the dump. After a disaster, restore the dump, then replay the binary log with mysqlbinlog up to one second before the mistake. Finish by checking the row count and the reconciliation view. The commands are in docs/RECOVERY.md. Decide beforehand whether to demo it live (takes several minutes) or just describe it; the safe choice is to describe it and show the file.
