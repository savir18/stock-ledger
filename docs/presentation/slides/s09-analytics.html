Slide 9 (about 60 seconds). Because history is kept, analytics are just queries on it. The running balance is a single window function. ABC classification ranks products by sales value and uses a cumulative SUM OVER to find the products making up the first 80 percent. Dead stock finds items with stock but no recent sale. Stock as of a date is impossible in an overwrite design and trivial here. The forecast is deliberately simple and explainable: a four-week moving average and a straight-line trend from SUM aggregates, with a recursive CTE producing the 12-week calendar. No machine learning. Live demo: Analytics page, and the running-balance chart on Ledger history.
